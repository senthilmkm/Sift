Support Hub & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Smart Profiles, PII redaction security, CPA tax exports, supported document types, and local storage.

🎒 What are Sift Smart Profiles and how do I switch between them?

Sift provides 5 specialized Smart Profiles designed for specific real-world paperwork:

  • 🎒 School & Family: Field trip permission slips, PTA signups, spirit days, tuition bills.
  • 🩺 Elder Care & Health: Doctor appointment fasting prep, lab orders, Rx refills, Medicare claim appeals.
  • 🛠️ Trade Contractors & Small Business: Overdue invoices, municipal permits, material receipts, license renewals.
  • 🏡 Property & HOA: HOA violation compliance deadlines, tenant lease renewals, utility shutoff warnings.
  • ⚖️ Legal & Immigration: USCIS I-797C receipt dates, court summons appearance windows, DMV renewals.

You select your profile during onboarding and can switch anytime by tapping the Profile Pill in the top header.

📊 How do I export receipts and expenses for tax time or my CPA?

Under ⚙️ Settings ➔ Data Export, tap Export All Tasks to Excel (.CSV). Sift generates a clean spreadsheet listing Vendor Name, Date of Purchase, Total Amount Spent, Tax Category (Materials & Supplies, Vehicle & Fuel, Utilities & Repairs, Office & Admin, Professional Fees, or Uncategorized Expense), and Receipt Notes ready to send directly to your CPA or accountant!

🔒 Is my Social Security Number, Medicare ID, or bank info safe?

Yes! Sift runs an On-Device Local PII Redaction Engine directly on your iPhone before sending images for AI extraction. SSNs, Medicare MBIs, Credit Cards, Bank Routing Numbers, EINs, and USCIS A-Numbers are automatically masked locally with zero cloud transmission of sensitive IDs.

🚨 How do Apple Critical Alerts work for urgent deadlines?

For urgent medical pre-op fasting rules, court summons, or utility shutoffs, Sift can dispatch Apple Critical Alerts that play an audible alert even if your iPhone is set to Silent or Do Not Disturb. You can toggle this permission in iOS Settings.

📄 What file extensions and document types are supported by Sift?

Sift accepts documents sent via paper photo scan or iOS Share Sheet from Mail, Files, or Photos:

.PDF .JPG .JPEG .PNG .HEIC .HEIF .WEBP .DOCX .DOC .XLSX .XLS .TXT .RTF

📷 What happens if a document is blurry, handwritten, or multi-page?

Sift is equipped to handle difficult edge cases:

  • Blurry / Low-Light Scans: AI flags items with an orange Review Needed badge for human verification.
  • Handwritten Sticky Notes: Gemini parses cursive and marginal notes into raw handwriting snippets.
  • Multi-Page Documents: Summarizes main action item dates while capturing secondary milestone dates.
  • Offline Mode: Photos are saved to local SQLite and automatically synced when cellular/Wi-Fi signal returns.

⏰ How does Sift calculate reminder notification times?

Sift uses precise mathematical logic for reminder triggers. For morning appointments (e.g. 9:00 AM doctor or court dates), Sift sets default alerts to 19:00 (7:00 PM) the evening prior so you can prepare overnight.

💳 What is the pricing model across profiles?

Sift uses a standard freemium model across all profiles: 5 free document scans per month total across all 5 Smart Profiles combined. Sift Pro ($4.99/mo or $39.99/yr) unlocks unlimited scans, critical alarms, CPA tax export tools, and multi-device sync.
