Support Hub & Frequently Asked Questions

Need help with Sift? Find answers to common questions about document scanning, file formats, notification alarms, and subscriptions below.

📄 What file extensions and document types are supported by Sift?

Sift accepts almost all document and image formats sent in school newsletters, email attachments, or web portals via the iOS Share Sheet and Camera scan:

.PDF .JPG .JPEG .PNG .HEIC .HEIF .WEBP .DOCX .DOC .XLSX .XLS .TXT .RTF

📥 How do I share an email attachment from Gmail or Mail into Sift?

Open any PDF or flyer attachment in Gmail, Apple Mail, or Files ➔ tap the iOS Share button ➔ select Sift. Sift will open automatically and extract your actionable items with Gemini AI!

Pro Tip: Scroll to the far right of your share icons, tap More (...), tap Edit, and tap + next to Sift to pin Sift as your #1 Favorite Share Target.

📷 How do I scan paper flyers on my fridge?

Tap the center 📷 Quick Scan button in Sift ➔ choose Take Photo ➔ snap a picture of your paper flyer. Gemini AI sifts through the document and presents your 15-second tap-to-confirm screen.

⏰ How do I customize reminder times or set urgent alarms?

In ⚙️ Settings ➔ Reminders & Notifications, use the interactive vertical scroll wheel to set your preferred default reminder time (e.g. 7:30 PM Night Before or 8:00 AM Same Day). For high-importance deadlines, tap the ⚡ Lightning Bolt icon on any task card to enable a high-priority urgent alarm.

✏️ Can I edit the due date or title after saving an item?

Yes! Tap the pencil ✏️ edit icon on any task card in the Actionable or Informational tab to open the Edit Item Modal. You can update the title, due date, personal notes, or switch between Actionable and Informational tabs.

🧹 How does auto-data retention work?

Under ⚙️ Settings ➔ Auto Data Retention & Deletion, choose how long completed items are kept (1w, 2w, 4w, 90d, 180d, or Never). Sift automatically cleans up old records to keep your phone clutter-free.

💳 How do subscriptions & payments work?

Sift offers 5 free document scans per month. Sift Pro unlocks unlimited Gemini AI document scans, urgent alarms, spouse email sharing, and CSV/PDF export. Managed safely via your Apple ID in iOS Settings.
