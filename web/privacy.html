Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 | Version 2.0 (Smart Profiles)

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Sift ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to simplifying paperwork management across 5 Smart Profiles (School & Family, Elder Care & Health, Trade Contractors, Property & HOA, and Legal & Immigration). Sift turns physical notices, forms, receipts, and digital PDFs into actionable items while enforcing zero cloud data retention.

2. On-Device Local PII Redaction Engine

To protect your sensitive financial, personal, and healthcare identifiers, Sift runs a local On-Device PII Redaction Engine directly on your iOS device before any image or text is transmitted to AI services:

  • Redacted Identifiers: Social Security Numbers (SSN), Medicare Beneficiary Identifiers (MBI), Credit Card Numbers, Bank Routing Numbers, Employer Identification Numbers (EIN), and USCIS Alien Registration Numbers (A-Numbers).
  • Local Execution: Pattern matching and redaction execute in memory on your phone. Unredacted sensitive numbers never touch cloud servers.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing (Google Gemini API)

Sift uses Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API via a secure proxy to extract document titles, due dates, action steps, and notes.

🔒 Zero Cloud Retention Guarantee:

  • Scanned image data is processed transiently in server memory solely to generate JSON response data.
  • Your documents and images are NEVER stored on external servers or used to train Google or third-party AI models.
  • No user document photos are retained in cloud database storage.

4. Local SQLite Database Storage

All parsed action items, profile preferences, custom notes, and app settings are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database (`sift_v2.db`). Your data is backed up only via your personal iOS iCloud backup settings.

5. iOS Permissions & Critical Alerts

Sift requests minimal iOS permissions:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used strictly to select or photograph documents within the app.
  • Notifications & Critical Alerts: Used locally to schedule reminder alarms for deadlines. Critical Alerts allow urgent healthcare or court notifications to play even when the phone is on Silent.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Sift is designed for adults, families, professionals, and caregivers. We do not knowingly harvest or collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Data Control & Complete Reset

You can edit, archive, or permanently delete items at any time. A full app data wipe is available via Settings > Reset App Data.
