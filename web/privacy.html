Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Version 1.2

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Sift ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to simplifying family organization while safeguarding user privacy. Sift is designed to scan paper flyers, notices, bills, forms, and digital PDF documents to extract actionable deadlines, fees, and event reminders. We believe your personal documents belong solely to you.

2. Data Collection & iOS Permissions

Sift requests minimal iOS permissions required to deliver document scanning and reminder alerts:

  • Camera Access: Used exclusively when you take a photo of a physical paper flyer or newsletter within the app.
  • Photo Library Access: Used when you import existing photos or documents from your iOS Camera Roll.
  • Notifications Permission: Used to deliver localized background push notifications and urgent alarms for approaching due dates.

We do not track your location, gather contact lists, sell personal data, or utilize cross-site advertising trackers.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Processing (Google Gemini API)

To extract task titles, dates, links, and actionable details from complex multi-page flyers or images, Sift utilizes Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API via our secure Cloudflare Worker Proxy server.

🔒 Zero Data Retention & AI Disclaimers:

  • Image data transmitted to the Gemini API is processed transiently in server memory to extract text and JSON structured data.
  • Your document photos and PDFs are NEVER used to train Google or third-party AI models.
  • No scanned images or parsed text are retained on cloud databases or external servers.

4. On-Device SQLite Local Storage

All extracted task items, titles, due dates, custom notes, archived items, and app settings are stored locally on your iOS device using SQLite database storage. Your data remains on your phone and is backed up only according to your personal iOS iCloud device backup settings.

5. AI Accuracy & Human-in-the-Loop Review

Artificial Intelligence algorithms (including Optical Character Recognition and Natural Language Understanding) parse document context with high accuracy. However, AI models may occasionally misread handwritten text, low-resolution flyers, or complex layout formatting. Sift incorporates a mandatory 15-Second Confirmation & Edit Screen, allowing you to review, edit, or correct extracted titles and due dates before committing items to your task database.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Sift is designed for busy professionals, families, students, and teams. We do not knowingly collect, request, or harvest personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Scanned flyers are processed strictly for local task organization.

7. Data Control & Deletion

You maintain 100% control over your stored data. You can edit, archive, or permanently delete tasks at any time within the app. You may also execute a full app data wipe at any time via Settings > Reset App Data.
